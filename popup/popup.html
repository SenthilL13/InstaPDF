How to Use

  1. Copy the link of any Instagram post, carousel, or reel.
  2. Click Paste Link or Use Active Tab.
  3. If the post contains direct PDF documents, click Download PDF.
  4. To compile carousel slides, choose your layout and click Download Carousel as PDF.

Platform Privacy & Security

InstaPDF complies strictly with platform security. It never requests your password, never attempts unauthorized scraping, and operates 100% locally inside your browser.
